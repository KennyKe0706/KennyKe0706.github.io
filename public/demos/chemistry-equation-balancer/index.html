Chemistry Equation Balancer

Enter an equation such as Fe + O2 -> Fe2O3 to find its smallest whole-number coefficients.

Scope: balances neutral equations by mass. Parentheses and hydrates (· or .) are supported; ions, charges, redox reactions, state labels, and multiple arrows are not.
How it works
  • Counts the elements in each formula.
  • Builds an element-conservation matrix.
  • Solves a nullspace vector with exact rational row reduction.
  • Scales the result to the smallest whole-number coefficients.

Remove state labels such as (aq) and do not enter pre-existing coefficients.
